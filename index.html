Minimum Inventory Requirements (MIR) — DOE Circular DC 2003-01-001

The Department of Energy enforces statutory minimum stockholding obligations to safeguard the archipelago against maritime supply disruptions:

  • Domestic Refiners (Petron Bataan): Minimum 30 days of continuous crude supply and finished petroleum products (~14.8 MB total).
  • Bulk Fuel Importers: Minimum 15 days of operational product stocks in usable commercial shore tanks (~7.4 MB total).
  • LPG Bulk Importers and Marketers: Minimum 7 days of commercial pressurised/cryogenic inventory (~0.45 MB total).
